International capability

Filing beyond India

A growing share of our clients need protection in more than one jurisdiction — an Indian manufacturer expanding into the UK and EU, or a UK brand entering the Indian market. Rather than handing that work to an unfamiliar correspondent firm, our Manchester office lets us coordinate international trademark and patent filing strategy directly, working with local associate counsel where needed for jurisdictions outside India and the UK.

In practice, this means a single point of contact for a multi-country filing programme, consistent class and specification strategy across jurisdictions, and status updates that don't get lost in translation between firms.

Filing in jurisdictions outside India and the UK is coordinated with local associate counsel as required by that jurisdiction's rules of practice.
