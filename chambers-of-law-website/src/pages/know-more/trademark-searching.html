Trade Mark Searching

The clearance search is the single step most likely to save you time, money, and a rejected application.

Why search before you file

Filing a trademark application without a prior search means finding out about conflicting marks only after the Registry issues an objection — or worse, after a competitor opposes your application or later sues for infringement. A proper search identifies these risks while a name or logo is still easy to change.

What a thorough search covers

Beyond a simple identical-match lookup on the public IP India portal, a proper clearance search considers phonetic and visual similarity to existing marks, related goods/services classes where confusion could arise, marks that are pending but not yet published, and common-law use that may not appear in the Registry database at all.

Why use an attorney-led search

Free public search tools are a useful first pass, but they don't assess likelihood of confusion the way an examiner or court would — that judgment call is where an attorney-led search adds the most value, particularly for marks that are similar-but-not-identical to existing registrations.

No search can guarantee an application will be free of objection or opposition; it substantially reduces — but cannot eliminate — that risk.
