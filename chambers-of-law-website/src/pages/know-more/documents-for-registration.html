Documents for Registration

What you'll need on hand before we file your trademark application.

Standard requirements

  • Applicant details — full legal name and address of the individual, partnership, or company applying, along with proof of identity for individuals.
  • Business proof — certificate of incorporation, partnership deed, or GST/business registration, where the applicant is an entity rather than an individual.
  • Representation of the mark — a clear image of the logo or wordmark exactly as it will be used, in the required format.
  • Goods/services specification — a description of what the mark will be used for, mapped to the relevant Nice Classification class(es).
  • Power of attorney (Form TM-48) — signed authorisation for your attorney to file and prosecute the application on your behalf.
  • User affidavit — if the mark is already in use, a signed affidavit with supporting evidence (invoices, packaging, dated marketing material) establishing the claimed date of first use.
  • MSME/Startup certificate — where applicable, to claim the reduced government filing fee available to individuals, startups, and small enterprises.

What we help you prepare

Not every applicant has every document ready, and that's normal — part of our engagement is helping you assemble what's genuinely required versus what's merely helpful, and drafting the user affidavit and specification language so the application is filed correctly the first time.

Exact document requirements can vary by applicant type and filing basis. This list is a general starting point, not an exhaustive checklist for every case.
