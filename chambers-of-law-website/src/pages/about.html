About the firm

Built on precision, kept small on purpose.

Chambers of Law has spent two decades helping Indian and international clients register, defend, and enforce the intellectual property their businesses are built on.

Our story

Chambers of Law was established in 2004 as a boutique practice focused on trademark, patent, copyright, and design law — at a time when intellectual property was still a niche concern for most Indian businesses. Two decades on, IP has moved to the centre of how companies compete, and our practice has grown with it, without losing the qualities that shaped it at the start: partners who stay hands-on with every matter, and a preference for doing fewer things well over spreading thin.

Today the firm operates from Ludhiana, India, with a second office in Manchester, United Kingdom, giving clients a genuine cross-border capability for filing and enforcement strategy rather than a single-market practice that occasionally handles international work. Our client base spans FMCG, pharmaceuticals, technology, manufacturing, fashion, and hospitality — sectors that differ enormously in how they use IP, but rely on the same disciplined process to protect it.

Our mission

We exist to make intellectual property protection accessible, understandable, and genuinely useful to the businesses that hold it — not just a certificate in a drawer, but a working asset that survives challenge, supports growth, and is worth what it says it's worth. That means clear advice before a filing is made, meticulous work while it's in process, and a readiness to litigate when a right is worth defending.

Our values

Integrity first

We give the advice a matter deserves, not the advice that's easiest to sell — including telling a client when a filing isn't worth pursuing.

Meticulous craft

Class strategy, specification drafting, and office-action responses are handled by attorneys who read every word before it's filed.

Genuinely global

Our Manchester office isn't a referral arrangement — it's how we practise cross-border strategy natively, on both ends.

Our approach

Every engagement starts with understanding what a mark, invention, or creative work is actually worth to the business behind it — because that shapes everything downstream, from which classes to file in, to how aggressively to pursue an opposition, to whether litigation is the right tool at all. We keep clients informed at each stage rather than going quiet between filings, and we staff matters so a partner is genuinely reachable, not a name on a letterhead.

Our expertise

Prosecution & registration

Trademark, patent, copyright, and industrial design filings — from clearance search through certificate of registration, including responses to office actions and examination reports.

Litigation & enforcement

Opposition and cancellation proceedings before the Registry and Intellectual Property Division, plus infringement and passing-off actions before the civil courts.
