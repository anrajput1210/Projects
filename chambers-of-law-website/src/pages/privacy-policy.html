This Privacy Policy explains what information Chambers of Law ("we", "us", "the firm") collects through this website, how it's used, and how it's protected. It applies to chambersoflaw.co.in and does not apply to information you share with us through other means, such as in-person meetings, phone calls, or email outside this site.

What we collect

We collect only the information you actively submit to us:

  • Contact form submissions — the name, email address, subject, and message content you provide when using the contact form on this site.
  • Local storage preferences — a small marker saved in your browser (via localStorage) to remember that you've acknowledged the disclaimer notice, and, where applicable, your light/dark appearance preference. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising pixels on this site as currently built.

How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry and, where you engage us, to open and manage that matter. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party processing

Contact form submissions are processed via Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, in order to deliver your message to us by email. Formspree's own privacy practices govern how it handles data in transit; see their published privacy policy for details.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry or, where a matter is opened, in accordance with our professional record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

You may request that we delete information you've submitted through this site, provided we are not required to retain it for legal, regulatory, or professional obligation reasons. To make a request, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date above.
